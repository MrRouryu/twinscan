## 1. r/NewTubers / r/PartneredYoutube (feedback post)

**Title:** I'm building a tool that checks whether your uploads look like one template. Would this actually help?

I run a few faceless channels. Since YouTube started calling mass-produced or repetitive uploads "inauthentic content", I keep worrying about one thing: I can't see my own template. Every video feels different while I'm making it.

So I'm building a small checker. You paste a channel and it compares your last 10–50 uploads with each other: thumbnail layout and colours, title structure, description boilerplate, length and posting rhythm. It shows which videos are near-twins and which part to change.

It won't predict what YouTube decides, because nobody can. It's a mirror.

Two honest questions:
1. What do you currently do to check this, if anything?
2. Would a weekly re-check be worth a few dollars a month to you, or is a one-off scan enough?

## 2. r/SideProject / r/indiehackers (build in public)

**Title:** Validating before building: a "sameness score" for YouTube channels

YouTube's monetization policy now names templated, mass-produced uploads as inauthentic content. Faceless and AI-assisted creators are nervous, and most of the "audit" tools out there give general advice.

My bet is that numbers beat vibes. The tool compares every upload with every other one and gives a score, with the closest pairs shown side by side. The paid part would be a weekly watch plus a pre-upload check.
